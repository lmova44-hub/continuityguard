Recovery workflow

Follow the sequence to demonstrate recoverability.

3-STEP EXERCISE
01

Create a verified backup

Copy the synthetic service database and verify its SQLite integrity and SHA-256 checksum.

BACKUP + INTEGRITY CHECK
02

Simulate a service outage

Mark the synthetic service unavailable and clear only its sample rows to test the restore process.

DESTRUCTIVE TO SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY
03

Restore from the latest backup

Restore the verified backup, validate database integrity, and record estimated RTO/RPO values.

Recovery concepts

What this exercise demonstrates

⛨
Integrity verification

SQLite integrity check and SHA-256 file checksum.

◷
RTO — Recovery Time Objective

Elapsed time from simulated outage to successful restore.

◴
RPO — Recovery Point Objective

Approximate age of the latest sample data at outage start.
